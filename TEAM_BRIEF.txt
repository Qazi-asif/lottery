SCRATCHCREST — TEAM BRIEF
For content research, copy, and new teammates
Updated: September 2026

============================================================
1. WHAT THIS IS (ONE PARAGRAPH)
============================================================

ScratchCrest is a subscription software product for Texas
convenience stores and gas stations that sell lottery scratch
tickets. We do not sell hardware kits, computers, or Fire
Sticks. The retailer uses a TV they already own plus a
touch-screen smart POS at the counter.

The product does three jobs in one account:
  1) Ticket inventory (packs in, tickets out, live counts)
  2) Selling on a touch display (smart POS — tap a game, set
     quantity, confirm). This is NOT a barcode-scan checkout.
  3) A live in-store TV board that shows games, prices, and
     what is almost gone, so customers can point at the wall.

We are a multi-store SaaS (one retailer can run many locations),
not a one-off app for a single shop.

2. LANGUAGE WE USE NOW (READ THIS FIRST)
============================================================

Do NOT describe ScratchCrest as a “scan system,” “scan-to-sell,”
or “one scan per sale.”

That was an earlier idea. The cashier product is now:

  TOUCH DISPLAY  +  SMART POS

Preferred words:
  - Touch POS / smart POS / touch display at the counter
  - Tap a game, choose quantity, confirm the sale
  - Live ticket wall / digital board / in-store TV display
  - Receive a pack, activate it, sell from the grid

Avoid in new research, ads, blogs, and scripts:
  - Barcode scanner, USB scanner, wedge scanner
  - “Scan the ticket,” “one scan,” “scan screen”
  - Hardware we ship or install

The live marketing site still has leftover “scan” wording in
places. Treat that as outdated copy to replace, not as the
product story.

4. HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS (STORE DAY)
============================================================

A. Sign up
   Owner picks a plan, pays on Stripe, sets a password from
   a one-time secure link. Adds a store (location).

B. Inventory
   A physical pack of scratch tickets arrives at the store.
   Owner/manager RECEIVES the pack in ScratchCrest, then
   ACTIVATES it. Activation creates every ticket in that pack
   in the system and puts the game on the sell grid and the TV.

C. Sell (smart POS — this is the counter)
   Cashier opens Sell. They see a touch grid of live games
   (price, name, tickets left). They tap a game, set how many
   tickets (starts at 0 — must pick a quantity), confirm.
   The sale is recorded, commission is locked in at that
   moment, stock drops, and the TV board updates.

   Cashiers only get Sell. They do not see billing, team,
   or owner reports.

D. Customer-facing TV
   Any TV opens a display URL for that store. Games, prices,
   hot / almost-gone, English and/or Spanish. Counts move as
   the POS sells. Official Texas Lottery artwork is OFF by
   default and only turns on if the account is licensed.

E. After the sale
   Sales log: when, game, pack, store, ticket, price,
   commission, who sold it.
   Shifts: open/close, cash vs sales vs payouts.
   Alerts: low stock, games that should have closed, unusual
   sell volume.
   Multi-store owners can compare locations and transfer a
   pack between stores.

F. Prize payouts
   Separate from a sale: cashing a winning ticket for a
   customer (cashing bonus). Do not mix this up with “we sold
   a ticket.”

7. RULES FOR CONTENT AND RESEARCH
============================================================

- Audience is Texas lottery retailers, 18+ only.
- Do not use official Texas Lottery logos, game art, or
  trademarked ticket faces unless we say the account is
  artwork-licensed. Default story: plain-text digital board.
- Never describe us collecting card numbers. Payments are
  Stripe Checkout only.
- Never describe emailing a plaintext password.
- Money in the product is cents (internal). In copy, use
  normal dollars.
- Do not promise we pick “lucky” games or beat the lottery.
  We track stock, sales, and commission.

Useful research angles:
  Why plastic dispensers fail / why a live wall sells more
  Touch POS vs old clipboard inventory
  Multi-store owners comparing locations
  Shift close and theft / double-sell protection
  English + Spanish on a store TV
  Software-only vs companies that sell a hardware kit

8. GLOSSARY (USE THESE WORDS)
============================================================

Tenant     The retailer account (the business)
Location   One physical store
Game       A scratch game (name, price, top prize)
Pack       One book/brick of tickets for a game, at a store
Ticket     One ticket inside a pack
Sale       Tickets sold on the touch POS (not a prize cash-out)
Payout     Cashing a winner for a customer
Commission What the store earns on a sale (typically 5%)
Display    The TV page customers look at
Sell / POS The cashier touch grid
